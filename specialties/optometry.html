What optometry patient intake typically includes

A standard new-patient visit at an optometry clinic generates several documents: a health and ocular history form, an insurance authorization, a HIPAA notice, a contact lens history for contact lens patients, and a release of records for patients transferring from another provider. For returning patients, there's usually a medication and prescription review on top of that.

Each of those forms asks for the same patient demographics — name, date of birth, address, insurance ID, emergency contact. Without automation, a staff member retypes all of it into every form separately. Sorta maps the patient's submission across every uploaded template simultaneously, so all of it fills at once.

You upload your existing optometry intake forms to Sorta, patients complete intake on their phone before their visit, and staff clicks sync — every form fills automatically. You don't replace your existing forms or your EHR. Sorta sits on top of both.
