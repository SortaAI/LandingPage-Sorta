How We Score Properties

Cost per Square Foot Analysis

We compare each listing's price-per-sqft against the ZIP code median, metro median, and comparable sold properties within 0.5 miles over the last 6 months. Properties priced 15%+ below the area median flag as potential value opportunities.

Source: NTREIS, HAR, SABOR, ACTRIS MLS feeds

Neighborhood Desirability Index

Composite score (1–100) combining: school ratings (GreatSchools API), crime statistics (FBI UCR data), walk/transit scores, 3-year property appreciation rate, median household income growth, and new development permits filed.

Source: Census ACS, FBI UCR, GreatSchools, CoStar

Repair Cost Estimation

AI model trained on 50K+ Texas inspection reports estimates repair needs based on: year built, last renovation date, listing photos (roof, HVAC, foundation indicators), days on market, and price reductions. Validated against actual rehab costs from investor networks.

Source: Property inspection databases, contractor bid aggregators

Investment-to-Return Projection

Cash-on-cash return calculated using: projected rent (Rentometer + local comps), estimated operating expenses (taxes, insurance, maintenance, vacancy at 8%), and financing assumptions (25% down, current 30-yr rate). Also models 5-year total return including appreciation.

Source: Rentometer, Zillow Rental Index, Freddie Mac PMMS

Multi-Unit Premium Scoring

Properties with 2+ units receive bonus scoring for: income density (gross rent multiplier), per-door acquisition cost vs. market, unit mix efficiency, and separate metering. Small multifamily (2–4 units) qualifies for residential financing rates — a key advantage.

Source: Texas MLS multi-family feeds, CoStar, county tax records
